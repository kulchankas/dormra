# Promotion channels — Vienna student communities

Places to post about Dormra when ready for growth. **Do not spam** — read group rules first, contribute value, disclose you're the founder.

## Facebook groups

| Group | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| [Erasmus in Vienna](https://www.facebook.com/groups/erasmusinvienna/) | Large exchange-student audience; housing posts common in Sep/Oct and Jan/Feb |
| [Vienna International Students](https://www.facebook.com/groups/viennainternationalstudents/) | Mix of degree + exchange students |
| [WG / Wohnung Wien](https://www.facebook.com/groups/wgwohnungwien/) | Competitive but high intent for housing |
| [Students in Vienna](https://www.facebook.com/groups/studentsinvienna/) | General student life |
| University-specific groups | TU Wien, Uni Wien, WU, BOKU, MedUni — search "[Uni] students Vienna" |

## Timing

- **Peak demand:** August–October (winter semester), January–February (summer semester intake).
- **Early bird:** May–July posts catch students before the rush.
- Post when live scraping covers a provider — ÖJAB listing without availability may disappoint.

## Suggested pitch (one-liner)

> Dormra tracks every major Vienna student dorm in one place and emails you when a room opens — free while we're in beta.
